Woodhouse Furniture — Returns and Refunds Policy (fictional example)

1. Return window. You can return most items within 30 days of delivery.
2. Condition. Items must be unused and in their original packaging.
3. Return shipping. If you return an item because you changed your mind, you pay the return shipping cost. If the item arrived damaged or defective, we pay for return shipping.
4. Refund timing. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 10 business days after we receive the returned item.
5. Non-returnable items. Custom-made furniture and mattresses that have been unwrapped cannot be returned.
6. Damaged deliveries. Report damage within 48 hours of delivery, with photos, through the order page.
